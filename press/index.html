What it is

GoAheadSpendIt is a simple weekly budget planner for iPhone and iPad. Users set a fixed 7-day Wallet, drag money Tokens onto category Tiles as they spend, review History, and see what remains before making the next choice.

Why it exists

Many budgeting apps focus on automation, bank sync, reports, and subscriptions. GoAheadSpendIt takes a quieter approach: a pay-once, private, visual weekly spending space built around clear choices instead of financial clutter.

Product promise

Short description

GoAheadSpendIt is a calm weekly budget planner that helps people decide before they spend, without ads, subscriptions, bank sync, accounts, or collected data.

Long description

GoAheadSpendIt gives users a simple 7-day Wallet, visual money Tokens, and category Tiles so they can see what remains before making the next spending choice. It is designed for people who want a private, pay-once budgeting tool rather than a bank-connected finance dashboard.
