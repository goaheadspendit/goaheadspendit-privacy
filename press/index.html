What it is

Will It Budget? is an allowance and money-skills app for iPhone and iPad. Users choose a weekly or monthly allowance period, organize purchases with suggested or custom Spending Tiles, and drag money tokens to record spending and see what remains.

Why it exists

Many finance apps begin with bank connections, dashboards, and subscriptions. Will It Budget? begins with a simpler question: will this purchase fit? Its visual, private approach helps make everyday money decisions easier to understand and discuss.

Editorial angles

Money skills for students and families

A practical learning app that makes everyday allowance choices visible, understandable, and easier to discuss.

Allowance management without bank sync

A manual, private alternative for families who do not want to connect financial accounts or create another login.

Learning by dragging and dropping

Money tokens and Spending Tiles turn abstract amounts into a direct action that users can see and understand.
