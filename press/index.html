What it is

GASI is a calm weekly budget planner for iPhone and iPad. Users set a 7-day Wallet, drag Tokens onto category Tiles, and see what their money still needs to cover before making the next spending choice.

Why it exists

Many budgeting apps focus on dashboards, automation, and subscriptions. GASI takes a quieter approach: simple weekly planning, visual spending awareness, and private on-device budgeting.
