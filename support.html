Will It Budget? Support

Money skills through everyday spending decisions.

Will It Budget? is an allowance and money-skills app for students and families. It uses a visual wallet, money tokens, and Spending Tiles to make everyday spending decisions easier to understand.

Quick Help

Common things to check

  • Open the app once before adding a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget.
  • Allowance periods can be weekly or monthly and can be changed in Settings.
  • Optional carryover keeps unused money available in the next allowance period.
  • Suggested or custom Spending Tiles can be added from the allowance screen.
  • Drag a money token onto a Spending Tile to record spending.
  • The Quick Guide, How It Works, and Budgeting Matters are available inside Settings.
Privacy

Private by design

Will It Budget? does not require an account or sign-in. Allowance and spending information remains stored locally on your device.

The app does not include advertising, subscriptions, third-party analytics, or behavioral tracking.
