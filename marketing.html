Decide before you spend.

GASI is a calm spending tool that places each choice inside a clear 7-day boundary, so you can see what your money still needs to cover before the next reset.

Calm weekly budgeting for iPhone and iPad

One wallet. Clear choices.

Your budget lives in one wallet. Tokens are your money. Tiles are your spending categories. As you spend, you move tokens onto tiles and make each choice visible, tangible, and easier to judge.

See the boundary before the purchase

Most budget apps show you where your money went. GASI helps you decide where it should go next. The ring shows the time remaining in your 7-day period, so spending is always seen in the context of what still has to be covered before the next reset.

Private by design

GASI works entirely on your device. No account. No analytics. No ads. No third-party tracking.
