Drag. Drop. Decide.

Money choices become easier to understand when you can see what happens after each decision.

1

Check your allowance

See what remains and how much time is left in the current weekly or monthly allowance period.

2

Choose a Spending Tile

Organize everyday purchases with suggested or custom tiles for the things that matter to you.

3

Move a money token

Drag a token onto a Spending Tile, record the purchase, and see how the decision changes your allowance.

Inside the App

A simple allowance tool designed to make everyday money decisions visible, understandable, and easier to discuss.

Visual money tokens

Use drag-and-drop tokens to practice spending decisions instead of only reading about budgeting.

Weekly or monthly periods

Choose the allowance rhythm that works best, with optional carryover for money that remains.

Helpful starter suggestions

Quickly begin with suggested Spending Tiles, or create custom tiles for personal priorities.

Budgeting Matters

Explore approachable educational content about choices, priorities, habits, and why budgeting matters.

iPhone, iPad, and widgets

Use the app across iPhone and iPad, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for helpful allowance information.

Made for students and families

Useful for personal allowance practice, family conversations, classroom discussions, and homeschool activities.
