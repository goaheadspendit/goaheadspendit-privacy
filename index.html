Privacy Policy for Will It Budget?

Deutsch

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

Will It Budget? is an allowance and money-skills app designed to help students and families practice thoughtful spending through everyday decisions.

The app uses a visual wallet, money tokens, and Spending Tiles to help you manage a weekly or monthly allowance period. Optional carryover allows unused money to remain available in the next allowance period.

Will It Budget? does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Data Collection

Will It Budget? does not collect, track, sell, or transmit personal data to the developer or to third parties.

Allowance amounts, spending history, Spending Tiles, preferences, and other app information remain stored locally on your device.

Widgets and Shared App Data

Will It Budget? uses an Apple App Group to share limited app information between the main app and its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

This shared information remains on your device and is used only to display details such as your remaining allowance, allowance-period timing, and Spend Signal. It is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Accounts, Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Will It Budget? does not include advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, social-media features, messaging, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Children’s Privacy

Will It Budget? does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

The app is designed so that allowance and spending information can remain private on the user’s device.

Your Control

You remain in control of the information stored by the app. You may use the reset options in Settings or remove the app from your device.

Because Will It Budget? does not collect app data on external servers, the developer does not retain a separate copy of your allowance or spending information.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
