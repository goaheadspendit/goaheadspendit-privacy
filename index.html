Privacy Policy for GASI

Deutsch

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

GASI is a personal budgeting app designed to help you decide before you spend. It gives you a calm, visual way to manage spending within a clear 7-day boundary. It does not provide financial or investment advice.

Data Collection

GASI does not collect, track, transmit, or store personal data outside your device. Your budget data remains stored locally on your device.

Accounts and Tracking

The app does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking technologies.

Your Control

You remain in control of your data. You may delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored information.
